KGB PDF Scanner & Organizer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

KGB PDF Scanner & Organizer is an iPhone and iPad app for scanning, editing, organizing, and backing up PDF documents. It is designed to keep users in control of their documents.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information, run advertising, or include developer-operated analytics or tracking services. The developer does not receive your scanned PDFs or imported documents.

Storage of documents

PDFs created or imported in the app are saved locally in the app's storage on your device. The app does not automatically upload your PDFs to a server operated by the developer. Your device's own backup settings, including optional Apple backup services, may separately affect how device data is backed up.

Camera, photos, and files

The app uses the device camera when you choose to scan documents. You can also select files or images to import. These inputs are used to perform the actions you request in the app.

Exporting, backing up, and sharing

You can choose to export or share individual PDFs and ZIP backups using Apple's Files and sharing features. When you choose an external application or cloud service as a destination, that service's own privacy practices apply to the information you provide to it.

Data retention and deletion

Your documents remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app, subject to any separate device or exported backups. Deleting the app may remove all PDFs stored only inside it. To preserve your collection, export a ZIP backup and save it outside the app before removing it.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, email kbfile@cox.net.
